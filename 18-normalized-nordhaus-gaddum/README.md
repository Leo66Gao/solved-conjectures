# Normalized algebraic-connectivity Nordhaus–Gaddum bound

AI-assisted research draft. Independent review and priority assessment are pending.

The manuscript gives a proof of Conjecture 2 of Kempton, Zaitzeff and Muthuprakash. It also gives a family attaining the order of the deficit and a sharp inequality for regular graphs.

[PDF](paper.pdf) · [LaTeX](paper.tex)

## Reproducing the checks

With NumPy and NetworkX installed, run `python3 verify.py`. It tests the matrix inequalities on 739 graph-atlas entries and evaluates the deficit for six orders in the sharpness family. These are floating-point checks.
