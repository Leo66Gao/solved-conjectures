# Two copies control all quantum-channel moments

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

For an arbitrary ensemble of finite-dimensional quantum channels, the synchronous tensor moments have the same largest eigenvalue modulus, after deleting one forced unit eigenvalue, for every order at least two. This is a spectrum statement, not an all-orders bound on the one-step operator norm. The Haar-Stinespring applications include exact maximal correlation between outer marginals and the $3/10$ projective-state gap of a three-qubit circuit. They do not identify the full unitary-design gap.
