# Small metrics with increasing magnitude

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

[Every four-point metric](FOUR_POINT.md) has strictly increasing magnitude. The [nine-point counterexample](../NINE_POINTS.md) therefore puts the minimum between five and nine. Two separate exact coefficient verifiers are provided.

[Unweighted graph metrics through eight points](GRAPH_METRICS.md) also have strictly increasing magnitude whenever they are of negative type. This is not a classification of arbitrary weighted graphs or metrics.
