# Prior-art cross-check

The lower construction is **not** claimed to be a new coordinate-refinement method. Lujia Wang, [*The frustration index of fully frustrated hypercubes*, §2, Lemma 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14906v1#S2), explicitly replaces a coordinate of weight 2b with four of weight b. Brass, Harborth and Nienborg had already reached the unrestricted density (1+1/√n)/2 at powers of four. Wang's second, two-coordinate refinement and integral arguments go beyond the quadratic construction in some dimensions, notably 9. Neither the four-way operation nor those unrestricted numbers are the proposed advance here.

The part for which we found no prior statement is the upper bound on the **whole** square-free, direction-wise quadratic class, via positivity of quadratic Walsh bias on a derivative slice of a derivative slice, followed by its exact equality with the four-ary code optimization in every dimension. Bialostocki had the unweighted spectral-scale bound for two-colorings assumed to contain no monochromatic square; here the corresponding two-color condition is derived only for above-half directions from a one-color prohibition. Claims of priority remain unverified pending a fuller literature search and specialist review.
