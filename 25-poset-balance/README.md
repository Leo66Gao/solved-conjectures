# Bounded-range progress on the 1/3–2/3 conjecture

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research draft · Independent review and priority assessment pending

A sharp gap-insertion inequality gives an additive improvement of order $1/D$ over the Brightwell–Felsner–Trotter balance constant for finite nonchain posets of incomparability range at most $D$. The paper also gives an exact rational prefix certificate for ruling out candidate counterexamples. The unrestricted $1/3$ bound remains open.

## Reproducing the checks

Run `python3 verify.py` with the Python standard library. It checks the gap-insertion inequality on 5,230 naturally labelled posets of orders two through six, with 32,899 applicable pair checks.
