# Power-of-two cycles in abelian lifts with at most six orbits

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

A sharp restricted result for [Erdős #64](https://www.erdosproblems.com/64). A minimum-degree-three graph with a free abelian action and at most five vertex orbits contains a cycle of length 4, 8, or 16. Six orbits require at most 32, and an explicit 912-vertex example requires 32. The unrestricted problem remains open.
