# Finiteness for elementary-positive spider graphs

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Not peer reviewed

For each fixed shortest leg at least two other than three, only finitely many graphs in the family are elementary-positive. The shortest-leg-three case is narrowed to two possible infinite progressions. Tom’s exact classification remains open.
