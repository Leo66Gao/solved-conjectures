# Scope and source audit (26 November 2026 UTC)

1. A. Hinrichs and J. Vybíral, [On positive positive-definite functions and Bochner's Theorem (authors' manuscript)](https://kmlinux.fjfi.cvut.cz/~vybirja2/publ/Vybiral19.pdf#page=11), §2.3, printed p. 11, Conjecture 6, displayed (14). [Published record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jco.2011.01.002). The page-indexed PDF confirms that both (14) and its subsequent Toeplitz restatement use the square of the signed total mass, not the ℓ² norm of the coefficient vector. Our sequences are real, even, finitely supported, and nonzero. Positivity of g means entrywise nonnegativity.
2. J. Vybíral, [An explicit counterexample to the Hinrichs–Vybíral conjecture (18 Sep 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21733v1), Theorem 1 and §§3–4. This refutes Conjecture 2; §3 differentiates the class of pointwise squares of positive-definite functions, and §4 describes a star Gram matrix. Its explicit theorem does not produce finite g≥0 and α on Z satisfying the reversed (14). Our interest in the integer problem was prompted by reading this paper; we do not claim independent discovery relative to its result. [Successor journal record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jco.2026.102097).
3. J. Vybíral, [A variant of Schur's product theorem and its applications](https://people.fjfi.cvut.cz/vybirja2/TUM-IBC/Vybiral_Advances.pdf), *Adv. Math.* 368 (2020), 107140. The conclusion for the much more special Novak/pointwise-square class is positive. Not contradicted.
4. D. Krieg and J. Vybíral, [New Lower Bounds for the Integration of Periodic Functions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00041-023-10021-7). In its convolution-squares application the coefficients of a *frequency* sum are convolution squares, furnishing a pointwise product in the kernel variable. Distinguish this from a nonnegative autocorrelation in the *spatial* integer variable, as in Conjecture 6.

Additional bounded searches included exact “Conjecture 6” and author names; nonnegative sequence, convolution lower bound and Toeplitz/translate formulations; the source paper title and later citations; targeted searches for published and AI-assisted claims on arXiv, Zenodo, GitHub and MathDB. No earlier solution of the exact displayed convolution problem was identified. We have not inspected every citing paper or every unindexed manuscript, so a firstness claim would be unjustified.
