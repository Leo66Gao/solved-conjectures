# A counterexample to Hinrichs–Vybíral’s convolution conjecture (Conjecture 6)

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

Two even integer sequences on the same 35 sites give squared convolution norm $3976$, below the conjectured lower bound $27900/7$ by $68/7$. Tensor powers also rule out every positive universal constant multiplying the original bound.

This is [Conjecture 6, equation (14)](https://kmlinux.fjfi.cvut.cz/~vybirja2/publ/Vybiral19.pdf#page=11) in the 2011 paper. [Jan Vybíral’s September 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21733v1) already disproved the broader Conjecture 2, not the discrete nonnegative convolution-factor statement. We do not claim the first disproof of Conjecture 2 or verified priority for Conjecture 6.
