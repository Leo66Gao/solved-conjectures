# Fractional planks in two-equal-normal planes

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

The $2/3$ lower bound holds at every feasible offset of $x+y+az=t$ if $0\le a\le5-2\sqrt6$ or $11/7\le a\le2$. Additional offsets are covered, and explicit examples show that equal-width triangular templates alone do not cover the general case. The theorem does not settle the middle range of coefficients.
