# How often are the witnesses in a perfect difference set late?

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

Partial results on [Erdős #1194](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1194). For every perfect difference set and every fixed exponent between one and two, the witnesses whose larger endpoints exceed that power of their difference have positive lower logarithmic density. The proof also quantifies a near-quadratic harmonic-window bound and shows by construction why lower ordinary density cannot replace logarithmic density. The worst-case endpoint-growth question remains open.
