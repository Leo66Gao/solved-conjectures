# Two further eight-point planar crescents

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research

The two real embeddings of an explicit configuration over the quadratic field with square root of 6 produce nonsimilar eight-point crescents. Neither is similar to any of the three shapes in [Jafari and Amin’s Table 11](https://doi.org/10.4236/ojdm.2016.63012). Both have one seven-point crescent subset and no six-point crescent subset.
