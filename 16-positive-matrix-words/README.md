# Spectral and trace positivity for real powers of positive definite matrices

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Not peer reviewed

A generalized word in positive definite matrices has universally positive spectrum exactly when its cyclically reduced sequence of letters and real exponents is invariant under reversal up to rotation. This proves [Hillar and Johnson’s generalized conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0504587) in standard form, including negative and irrational powers. The [ordinary-word classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02314v2) is due to Garbe and Wei.
