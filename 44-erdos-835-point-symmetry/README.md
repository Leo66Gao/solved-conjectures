# Point symmetry at the first open case of Erdős #835

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

A proper 17-coloring of the 16-subsets on 32 points cannot have a point-transitive symmetry group, even if symmetries may permute the colors. In fact its point-symmetry group has no subgroup of order 16. This rules out a symmetry class, not all colorings.
