# Exact all-noise complement-two theorem

**Theorem.** For every integer n≥2, every deterministic quantizer U of a uniform n-bit source with ≤2^(n−2) outputs, and every independent binary symmetric channel crossover p∈[0,1/2], one has I(U;Y)≤(n−2)C(p), with equality attained by reading n−2 coordinates. There is **no balance assumption**. This strengthens the k=n−2 high-noise result, but only covers the six-bit question at n=8.

Write C=ln 2−h(p) in nats, and J(A)=I(X_A;X_A xor Z), where X_A is uniform on the nonempty finite cube subset A. Let m=|A|. We will prove
> J(A) ≥ (7/2−6/m) C.

For m≥16 we prove the stronger bound J(A)≥(7/2)C. Indeed, the uniform distribution on A is the equiprobable mixture of uniform distributions on its 16-element subsets. The output entropy is concave under mixing and the conditional noise entropy n h(p) is constant, so the m=16 bound implies the m>16 assertion. For m=1 the displayed lower bound is vacuous. For m=2 the two different words differ in at least one coordinate; that coordinate alone, after the BSC, conveys C, exceeding the required C/2.

It remains to treat m=3,…,16, a finite dimension-independent calculation. Let t=7/2−6/m for m<16 and t=7/2 for m=16. By the two-point polarization argument in Section 2 of the manuscript there is a shifted downset of the same size with no larger J. Such a set uses at most m−1 coordinates: a point occupying the jth coordinate forces all earlier singleton words. Fixed coordinates affect neither J nor the bound. Thus for each candidate in its support dimension d we only need prove H(P_A)≥t ln 2+(d−t)h(p). Here P_A is its noisy output law. The four-point square has J=2C exactly, so it is separated. The remaining checks give strict inequalities on 0<p<1/2.

The standard-library exact certificate is **unbalanced2-all-p.py**, with result **unbalanced2-all-p.log**. It grows every shifted family by strictly increasing word index. Each candidate is an upward edge from an existing mask, obtained by putting a 1 in bit 0 or by moving one bit up one vacant position. Its required immediate predecessors, formed by the reverse moves, must all be already present. Completeness only needs every stable family to pass these tests. The cardinality-m candidate counts for m=3,…,16 (including the four-point square) are 1,2,2,3,4,6,7,10,13,18,23,31,40,54. In total **214** candidates.

**Upper-noise end cap.** As explained in Section 2 of the manuscript, set B_A=Σ_i(E_A χ_i)^2 and ρ=1−2p. D(P_A||uniform)≤ρ²[B_A+(2^d/m−1)ρ²]/[2(2−(1+ρ)^d)] by the Taylor upper bound and Parseval. For each nonexceptional shape the verifier checks, at ρ=2^−15, that the bracketed ratio without the ρ²/2 factor is <d−t. By monotonicity it holds for 0<ρ≤2^−15. Since C≥ρ²/2, this yields D(P_A||uniform)≤(d−t)C, exactly the desired output-entropy comparison. The smallest high-noise coefficient slack d−t−B_A is 1/64, occurring for m=16.

**Lower-noise end cap.** H(P_A)≥ln m, since the output is a mixture of translates of the uniform input set. For p≤2^−20, except at the four-point shapes, the certificate checks that this lower bound exceeds t ln 2+(d−t)h(2^−20); d−t≥0. For the noncube four-point shape, the 3-dimensional star, the outgoing boundary is 3/2 per point. The argument in Section 2 of the manuscript gives H(P_A)−ln 4≥(3/2)p(1−p)^2[ln(1/p)−ln 3]. With L=ln(1/p)≥13, c=(3/2)(1−2/2^20)>1 and c(13−11/10)>14, this lower bound is >p(L+1)≥h(p). This completes its low-noise check.

**Intermediate noise.** On [2^−20,(1−2^−15)/2], the verifier traverses a binary dyadic subdivision, separately for each candidate. Certify each closed [l,h] by H(P_A(l))>t ln 2+(d−t)h(h), using noise-entropy monotonicity. The stored exact integers at a/D (D=2^40) are w_y=Σ_{x∈A}a^dist(D−a)^(d−dist). Thus H(P_A)=ln m+Σ_y w_y ln(D^d/w_y)/(mD^d). The logarithms are rigorously enclosed after binary range reduction by the first 21 terms of the atanh series and the directed upper tail 2z^43/[43(1−z²)] at fixed-point scale 2^80 (every step rounded outwards). The script completed 52,760 closed interval checks across all nonexceptional candidates using only Python integers/fractions. For speed the independent cardinality ranges may run concurrently; they were checked as 3–10, 11–14, 15, and 16. The noiseless limit follows by continuity and p=1/2 is trivial.

Finally if N=2^n and K is the number of nonempty quantizer cells, average the affine bound over cells with probabilities m/N to obtain I(X;Y|U)≥(7/2−6K/N)C≥2C. Since I(X;Y)=nC and U is deterministic, subtracting yields the theorem. This addresses the next complementary endpoint after the known n−1 case (Huleihel–Ordentlich, 2017); external peer review and broader priority checking remain outstanding.
