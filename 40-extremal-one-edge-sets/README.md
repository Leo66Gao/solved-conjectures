# One-edge sets in trees

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

See [the proof](RESULT.md) for a complete resolution of Dossou-Olory–Andriantiana Conjecture 1, a classification of its maximizers and runners-up, and an extension to all k with n≥18k+3. [Verification code](verify.py) and [output](verification.txt) reproduce the finite cases and independent exact checks. No nonstandard Python packages are required.

The [connected-graph extension](CONNECTED.md) classifies the exact first 2k+3 connected graphs for n≥18k+3. See [connected verification](verify_connected.py) and [its output](connected-checks.txt).
 For k=1 the connected-graph maximizer is identified for every order, with a change from a subdivided star to a triangle with pendant leaves at n=7; see [finite verifier](connected_small.py) and [output](connected-small-checks.txt).
