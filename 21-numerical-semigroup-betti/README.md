# Betti numbers of numerical-semigroup tangent cones

AI-assisted research draft. Independent review and priority assessment are pending.

The manuscript presents a simplicial formula for graded and total Betti numbers, addressing Problem 1 of Moscariello and Sammartano. It gives two derivations and an explicit finite cutoff.
