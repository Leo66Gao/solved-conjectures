# Reliability zeros in high-girth, maximally connected graphs

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Independent review and priority assessment pending

All-terminal failure-probability roots remain dense in the unit disk even for simple regular bipartite graphs with vertex and edge connectivity equal to the degree and logarithmically large girth. A refined construction locates a separated, nearly regular polygon of innermost roots. Unrestricted simple-graph density was previously established by [Pjotr Buys](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07541); this result is about the extra restrictions and root geometry.
