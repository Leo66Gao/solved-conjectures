# Ehrhart roots of arbors

**Leo Gao** · AI-assisted research · Not peer reviewed

Singleton arbors of height at most two have only real Ehrhart roots. At height three, examples with a number of distinct nonreal pairs of order at least the square root of the dimension exist. The earlier disproof of the unrestricted real-root conjecture is credited.
